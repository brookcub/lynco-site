Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Brooklyn Rose Ludlow, doing business as LynCo ("LynCo," "we," "us"), builds software that respects your privacy by design. This policy explains what we do — and, mostly, do not — collect.

The short version

We do not collect your personal data. LynCo software has no user accounts, no telemetry, no analytics, and no advertising. We do not track you, profile you, or sell information about you. All power to the users.

Audio and microphone

Some LynCo applications process audio from your microphone or audio interface. All audio is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to LynCo or any third party. When an app requests microphone access, it is solely to process sound on your device for the app's stated function.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect or transmit: your name, email (except when you contact us directly — see below), location, device identifiers, usage statistics, contacts, or the content of your projects, recordings, or files. Your work stays on your device.

Optional crash reporting

If a LynCo app offers crash reporting, it is opt-in. Nothing is sent unless you explicitly choose to send it, and you can see what would be sent before deciding. We use this only to fix bugs, never to identify or track you.

When you contact us

If you email our support address, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us, and we use it only to respond to you and improve our products. We do not add you to marketing lists without your consent.

Purchases

When you buy LynCo software, payment is handled by an app store or an independent merchant of record acting as the seller of record. We never receive or store your full payment-card details. Those processors have their own privacy policies governing the checkout.

Changes

We may update this policy as our products evolve. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above. Because our baseline is "we collect nothing," changes will almost always be clarifications rather than new collection.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@lyncodsp.com.
